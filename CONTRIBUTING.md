# Contributing

Thanks for helping keep this index accurate!

## Reporting a wrong or missing award

[Open an issue](../../issues/new) with:

- the venue and year (e.g. `CSCW 2024`),
- the award, paper title, and authors,
- a link to the **official source** (conference awards page, proceedings front matter, or organizer announcement).

## Pull requests

Edit `data/awards.json` (the CSV is derived from it). Requirements:

1. **Official source URL required** for every entry. Third-party lists are not sufficient sources on their own.
2. Scope is Best Paper–class awards (Best Paper, Best Student Paper, Distinguished/Outstanding Paper, Test of Time, Impact Awards and equivalents) at social computing, HCI, web science, and computational social science venues, 2015 onward.
3. Author names as written in the official source.

Accepted changes are folded into the canonical dataset that powers
[casp.jp/social-computing-best-papers](https://casp.jp/social-computing-best-papers/) and are mirrored back here by the daily sync.
